# Data pipeline (ADR-0012) — Spike 7 slice

Builds the starting scenario from public data. Standard library only for now
(Polars/DuckDB come in when tables get big).

| Step | Script | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fetch | `fetch_eurostat.py` — downloads datasets listed in `sources.toml` via the Eurostat API (JSON-stat) | `data/raw/<id>.json` + `data/raw/<id>.provenance.json` (url, retrieval time, sha256, licence) |
| 1b. Normalise census | `normalise_census.py` — turns the two Census 2021 downloads into the population generator's margin file, filling suppressed cells | `data/derived/census2021_margins_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/census2021_margins_ro.json`) |
| 1c. Normalise education and activity | `normalise_census_attributes.py` — turns the two region-level Census 2021 downloads into the margin file of the generator's second stage, filling suppressed cells and checking them against the county margins | `data/derived/census2021_edu_activity_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/census2021_edu_activity_ro.json`) |
| 1d. Normalise jobs | `normalise_census_jobs.py` — two region-level Census 2021 tables (status in employment by industry group and by occupation) and two EU Labour Force Survey cross-tables used as patterns | `data/derived/census2021_jobs_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/census2021_jobs_ro.json`) |
| 1e. Normalise locality size and tenure | `normalise_census_housing.py` — two county-level Census 2021 tables (persons by size of locality, households by tenure) | `data/derived/census2021_housing_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/census2021_housing_ro.json`) |
| 1f. Normalise earnings | `normalise_earnings.py` — wages and salaries by industry group from the national accounts (2021-Q4), proportions by sex, occupation, age and education and the earnings deciles from the Structure of Earnings Survey 2022, the minimum wage | `data/derived/earnings2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/earnings2021_ro.json`) |
| 1g. Normalise pensions | `normalise_pensions.py` — the year's pension expenditure, the gender pension gap and a replacement ratio from Eurostat; the minimum pension and the number who receive it entered by hand (listed under `provenance.manual`); the curve's shape from the earnings file | `data/derived/pensions2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/pensions2021_ro.json`) |
| 1h. Normalise household wealth | `normalise_wealth.py` — the households' cash, deposits and loans from the financial accounts and their dwellings from the capital stock; Hungary's wealth shares as a check; every rule about who holds what is an educated guess, listed under `provenance.guesses` | `data/derived/household_wealth2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/household_wealth2021_ro.json`) |
| 1i. Normalise other incomes | `normalise_incomes.py` — households' mixed income and agriculture's surplus from the national accounts; how it splits into farm, self-employment and informal income, and which wages are undeclared, are educated guesses listed under `provenance.guesses` | `data/derived/other_incomes2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/other_incomes2021_ro.json`) |
| 1j. Normalise the input-output table | `normalise_io.py` — Eurostat's inter-country input-output table (FIGARO) for 2021: Romania's 64 industries, their purchases by origin (home, EU, rest of the world), final uses, exports and value added, in lei; plus the split of the 64 into the game's 90 industries (guessed shares) | `data/derived/io_table2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/io_table2021_ro.json`) |
| 1k. Normalise balance sheets | `normalise_balance_sheets.py` — Eurostat's financial accounts at the end of 2021: who holds and who owes each instrument (currency, deposits, debt securities, loans, equity, insurance and pensions, other accounts) for households, firms, banks, other financial firms, pension funds, government, central bank and the rest of the world | `data/derived/balance_sheets2021q4_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/balance_sheets2021q4_ro.json`) |
| 1l. Build firm units | `normalise_firms.py` — cohort firms by size class and foreign control, and public units, for each of the 90 industries: firm counts, employment, output, value added and wages, from structural business statistics and foreign affiliates statistics on the input-output table's totals | `data/derived/firm_units2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/firm_units2021_ro.json`) |
| 1m. Firm balance sheets | `normalise_firm_balance_sheets.py` — spreads the balance sheet of non-financial corporations and their fixed assets (plus households' business capital) over the non-financial cohort units; totals measured, spread guessed | `data/derived/firm_balance_sheets2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/firm_balance_sheets2021_ro.json`) |
| 1o. Normalise the budget | `normalise_budget.py` — general government spending 2021 by COFOG subgroup and ESA transaction, the national totals and GDP from Eurostat; occupied public posts (December 2021) and the cash execution entered by hand from the Ministry of Finance (`provenance.manual`); the split of employers' contributions and of health and social-care posts guessed (`provenance.guesses`). Checked and reconciled by `python/reference/budget_reference.py` (spec `economy/budget-2021`) | `data/derived/budget2021_ro.json` (a copy is committed as `fixtures/budget2021_ro.json`) |
| 1n. Assemble and check (interim) | `build_scenario.py --sample-scale N [--out DIR]` — runs every stage of the Python reference on the fixtures and reports whether the parts agree (wage bill, pensions, mixed income, household and firm balance sheets, employers, firm units against the input-output table, income against consumption, income distribution). Stands in for the Rust `build-scenario` of ADR-0018 until the later stages exist in Rust | `scenario.json`, `report.json` in `DIR`; exit 1 if a check fails |
| 2. Synthesise population | `econ-cli synth-population --margins FILE --sample-scale N --seed S --out DIR [--attributes FILE [--jobs FILE]] [--housing FILE]` (`just synth-population 100` runs every stage on the fixtures) — the population generator, Rust crate `econ-popgen`. Each further margin file (1c, 1d, 1e) runs one more stage and adds its columns | `households.arrow`, `persons.arrow`, `fit_report.json` in `DIR` (default `data/population/`) |
| Spike 7 slice | `synth_counties.py` — draws a seed sample and fits it to county marginals with IPF | `data/derived/population_<counties>.csv` |
| Codegen | `scripts/codegen_glossary.py` (repo root) — glossary → `crates/econ-types/src/glossary.rs` | checked in CI |

`data/` is git-ignored. `dvc.yaml` describes the same stages for DVC
(`uv tool install dvc`, then `dvc repro` from this folder; offline, skip the download with `dvc repro -s normalise_census synth_population`), so raw downloads can be cached
and versioned outside git.

```powershell
python python\pipeline\fetch_eurostat.py            # needs internet
python python\pipeline\synth_counties.py --fixture  # works offline
python -m unittest discover -s python\pipeline\tests
```

`fixtures/marginals_illustrative.json` is **illustrative, not Romanian data**. `fixtures/census2021_margins_ro.json` is real: Eurostat Census 2021 round (tables `cens_21cobhs_r3` and `cens_21hhcs_r3`, reuse allowed with attribution), all 42 counties. The population generator that reads it is specified in `docs/02-design/society/population-generator.md` and implemented in `crates/econ-popgen`; its Python reference is `python/reference/popgen_reference.py`, and the two are compared record for record by AC-POP-07.
Real marginals come from the Census 2021 tables (INS) and Eurostat; the base
sample from the IPUMS 2011 Romania 10% sample (private, never committed).
