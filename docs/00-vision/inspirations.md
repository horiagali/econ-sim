# Inspirations & Competitor Teardown

For each game: what it does well, what it does badly, what we take, what we avoid.

## Power & Revolution (Eversim, Geopolitical Simulator series)
- **Does well:** breadth of levers, real-world countries, feeling of running a state.
- **Weak spots to verify:** opaque economic model, unclear causality, UI overload. *(To research — gather player reviews/forums.)*
- **Take:** country-level scope, ministry-based policy structure.
- **Avoid:** _TBD_

## Democracy 4 (Positech)
- Policy-to-voter-group influence graph; very readable causality. Economy is shallow.

## Victoria 3 (Paradox)
- Pop-based economy, goods markets, buildings. Strong on production chains, weaker on finance/money.

## Tropico / Workers & Resources
- City/region-level production chains; useful for supply-side intuition.

## Non-game references
- Stock-flow consistent macro models (Godley & Lavoie).
- Agent-based macro models (e.g. Eurace, the "Mark-0" family).
- Central bank teaching simulators (e.g. "Chair the Fed").
