# Vision

## Elevator pitch
You run a country. You set taxes, budgets, interest rates, regulations and trade policy — and a living economy responds. Not with scripted events and opaque modifiers, but through mechanisms that mirror how real economies work: households spend and save, firms hire and invest, banks lend, prices adjust, capital flows, debt accumulates.

## What "better than Power & Revolution" means
See [inspirations](inspirations.md) for a detailed teardown. In short:
- **Causality you can see.** Every indicator can be drilled into to show what moved it and why.
- **Consistent accounting.** Every unit of money is someone's asset and someone else's liability. No free lunches, no leaks.
- **Emergent crises.** Inflation spirals, bank runs, debt crises and recessions arise from the system, not from dice rolls.
- **Policies have trade-offs and lags.** Cutting rates works — slowly, and with side effects.

## Target experience
- The player feels like a finance minister + central banker + head of government who must balance competing goals.
- Short-term pressure (budget, approval) vs long-term health (productivity, debt sustainability).
- Learning the game teaches real intuition about economics.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Real-time tactical military.
- Exact calibration to a specific real country (plausible shapes matter more than decimals).
- Multiplayer.
