# The budget of Romania in 2021 — spending by function and type, public posts and the public wage bill

## Question
What did Romania's general government spend in 2021, on what (by function, COFOG) and how (wages, goods and services, social benefits by programme, subsidies, interest, investment, other)? How many people did the state employ at the start date (1 December 2021), in which sectors, and for what wage bill? How do these numbers agree with the parts of the starting state already built (the firm units, the earnings file, the pension file, the input-output table, the starting population)?

## Short answer
- **Total spending 2021: 474.03 billion lei, 40.18% of GDP** (GDP 1,179.83 billion lei). Measured, Eurostat ESA 2010 general government accounts (`gov_10a_main`, `gov_10a_exp`). Confidence high.
- **By type:** compensation of employees 132.66 bn (wages 120.02 + employers' contributions 12.64); goods and services 70.48; social benefits 156.50 (cash 148.16 + in kind 8.34); subsidies 6.08; interest 17.32; investment 48.04 (fixed capital 48.18, net land sales −0.14); other 42.95 (current transfers 25.71 of which the EU budget 12.11, capital transfers 16.22, other taxes paid 1.02). They add up to the total exactly.
- **By function:** social protection 158.57 bn (old-age pensions 111.29), economic affairs 71.68 (transport 36.88), health 65.12, general public services 58.75 (of which debt 17.64), education 38.01, public order 31.84, defence 17.10, housing and amenities 13.60, culture 10.92, environment 8.45.
- **Public posts: 1,262,318 occupied in December 2021** (central 807,562, local 454,756), Ministry of Finance staffing statistics. Confidence high for the total and the big ministries; the split of health and social care is an educated guess.
- **Public wage bill: 120.02 bn lei of wages (D11)**, 7,923 lei a month per post on average. The firm units' public units pay 120.09 bn (0.07% more), but count 1.50 million persons (19% more than the posts) and split them very differently between health, administration and social care. The starting population has 1.06 million public employees paid 110.5 bn a year.

What the published figures do not give, and the guesses used for the game (owner's rule of 2026-10-11: educated guesses are fine; the total is kept exact):

> **Simplification:** health is 243,421 posts: the Ministry of Health's 18,421, plus 60,000 in hospitals and institutes run by ministries from own revenue (central), plus 165,000 in hospitals run by counties and towns (of the 176,625 local own-revenue posts). Romania's public hospitals were mostly handed to local authorities in 2010 and finance themselves from the health insurance house's contracts, which is why they appear as "own revenue". Confidence low.

> **Simplification:** social care is 130,000 local posts: the county directorates for social assistance and child protection and the personal assistants of people with severe disabilities, who are employed by town halls. Its wage bill (5.3% of public wages for 10% of the posts, about 4,100 lei a month) fits mostly low-paid personal assistants. Confidence low.

> **Simplification:** public order and justice is 178,548 posts: the Ministries of Internal Affairs and of Justice, plus 40,000 for courts, prosecutors and the intelligence and guard services, which the reports do not list. Confidence medium.

The rest of central administration (184,321 posts, including the Ministry of Finance) and of local administration (159,756) are what is left, so the total stays exact.

### Wages and employers' contributions by function
COFOG publishes compensation (D1) by function, not wages (D11) and contributions (D12) separately. The split matters: the ledger pays wages to persons, while employers' contributions of the state are paid by the state to itself (the 2.25% labour insurance contribution, CAM) or are imputed (no money moves).

> **Simplification:** employers' contributions are the CAM, 2.25% of wages in every function, plus imputed contributions of 9.94 billion lei, the counterpart of the special pensions paid from the budgets of defence and the interior (D622 is 9.60 billion). The imputed part is put 40% on defence and 60% on public order, in proportion to compensation inside each. Wages are then $(D1 - \text{imputed}) / 1.0225$, adjusted to add up to the published D11 exactly. Confidence low for the split between defence and order; medium for the total.

Resulting wages per post, a month (D11 / posts / 12): education 6,990 lei; health 9,271; defence 6,480; public order, central and local administration together 8,974; social care 4,099; all 7,923. For comparison, the national average gross wage in November 2021 was 5,944 lei (INS). Public pay above the average fits Romania after the 2017–2018 public pay law, which raised health and defence pay most. Wages per post include bonuses, the military's allowances and posts filled for part of the year, so they sit above survey earnings.

| Government consumption | P3 211.18 bn | input-output table, government final use 211.18 bn | 1.000 | agree |
| Public employees | 1,262,318 posts | starting population, 1:1000: 1,064,443; 1:100: 1,063,377 | 0.843 | the census counts fewer people working in public administration, education and health than the Ministry counts posts |
| Public wages | D11 120.02 bn | starting population, 1:1000: 110.49 bn; 1:100: 110.53 bn | 0.921 | follows from the employees |

By public unit the disagreement is large (firm file against budget): health 494,816 persons against 243,421 posts; social care 3,687 against 130,000; administration pays 45.92 bn against 56.28 bn. In the starting population the five public units pay very different wages per head (defence about 14,900 lei a month, administration about 6,300) because Stage J ranks units by pay per person and every public unit has the same pay per person in the firm file (the 12.5 guess), so their order is their order in the file. Both disappear if the public units are built from the budget's posts and wages.

## Implications for the game
- **[Budget 2021](../../02-design/economy/budget-2021.md):** the starting budget is a table of 13 lines (one per ministry line) by 12 economic types that adds up to the published totals exactly; the fixture `python/pipeline/fixtures/budget2021_ro.json` holds it.
- **[Fiscal policy](../../02-design/economy/fiscal-policy.md):** the ministry budgets, public wages and headcount levers start from this table; the deficit to finance at the start is about 7% of GDP.
- **[Industries](../../02-design/economy/industries.md) (the firm units):** the public units' persons and wages should come from the budget (posts and D11 by unit) instead of the guessed 12.5 persons per million lei; see the spec's open questions.
- **[Social transfers](../../02-design/economy/social-transfers.md):** the programme totals are the targets for the benefits the mechanics will pay; family benefits (19.3 bn) and disability benefits (10.6 bn) are much larger than unemployment benefit (0.6 bn).
- **[EU membership](../../02-design/economy/eu-membership.md):** the EU budget contribution is 12.1 bn lei a year; the deficit starts above the 3% reference value (Romania was in the excessive deficit procedure since 2020).
